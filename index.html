Prevalence of Smoking and its Impacts

Background Information and Findings

Smoking causes many health complications such as increased risk of cancer, heart disease, lung complications, and strokes. Approximately 1 out of 10 Australians smoke on a daily basis. The average life expectancy of some states are lower as a result of higher smoking rates. These states include Tasmania, Queensland, and the Northern Territory. This concludes that higher levels of smoking reduces life expectancy.

Which State is the most affected?

From the chart shown, we can see that Queensland is the state with the highest rate of daily smokers at 14%. On a daily basis, there are approximately 560 people who smoke in Queensland. The Northern Territory is just behind with 13.5% of the population being daily smokers.

Why is smoking bad?

Smoking cause and expedites health complications such as Cancer, Heart diseases and strokes, Chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD) and more. New South Wales suffers the most with the highest number of cases for Heart diseases and strokes. The disease has the highest number of cases for each of the states as compared to the other diseases.

Conclusion

Left unchecked, smoking can and will lead to health complications or worse. Queensland and the Northern Territory have a significantly higher rate of smokers, a lower than average life expectancy, and high number of cases for related diseases.
